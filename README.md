# C-Net Library

C-Net Library is a modern study-library ERP and digital learning platform for managing students, study slots, seats, memberships, fees, attendance, books, digital resources, enquiries, jobs, staff, reports, and CMS content.

## Production Target

- Domain: `cnetlibrary.mciedu.com`
- Hosting: BigRock
- Repository: `shekharnalanda/C-Net-Library`

## Planned Stack

- Laravel 12
- PHP 8.2+
- MySQL 8+
- Bootstrap 5
- Vite

## Core Modules

- Public website and CMS
- Online enquiry and admission
- Student management
- Study hall, slot, and seat management
- Membership and fee plans
- Payments, receipts, dues, refunds, and cashbook
- Attendance and QR-based entry/exit
- Physical library and digital library
- Job and career portal
- CRM and follow-ups
- Reports and analytics
- Staff, roles, permissions, and audit logs
- Multi-branch support
